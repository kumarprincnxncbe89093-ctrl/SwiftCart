Short version

Use only your own account details, protect your password and OTP, and do not misuse the platform, merchants, or other users.

SwiftCart may limit access when abuse, fraud, scraping, or harmful behavior is detected.

Allowed use

  • Browsing products, managing your account, placing orders, and using merchant tools where authorized.
  • Using the official SwiftCart login page and related support pages for account access.
  • Submitting accurate account, order, and merchant information.

Prohibited behavior

  • Attempting unauthorized access to other accounts, protected APIs, or owner-only tools.

Account responsibility

  • Keep your password and OTP private and use only official SwiftCart pages for sign-in.
  • Log out when using shared devices and review your account details regularly.
  • Report suspicious behavior through the public contact information when available.
