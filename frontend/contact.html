What to include in a support request

  • Your SwiftCart email address or user code.
  • Your order number, if the request is about a purchase.
  • A short description of the issue and the device you are using.

What support will never ask for

  • Your full account password.
  • Your banking password, UPI PIN, or OTP from another service.
  • Your debit or credit card CVV.
