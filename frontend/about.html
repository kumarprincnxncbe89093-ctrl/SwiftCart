About SwiftCart

Independent marketplace access with public trust and policy pages.

SwiftCart is an independent ecommerce marketplace project for product browsing, account login, carts, checkout, and merchant workflows. It is not affiliated with Amazon, Flipkart, or third-party brands referenced in catalog listings.

Password and OTP sign-in Public privacy and contact pages Distinct SwiftCart branding
Safe login expectations

The SwiftCart login page only asks for your SwiftCart email, user code, password, or one-time password.

We do not request debit card CVVs, UPI PINs, or net-banking passwords during sign-in.

What SwiftCart does

SwiftCart helps shoppers browse products, manage a cart, sign in securely, track orders, and access account details. Merchants can also manage products and review order activity from their own workspace.

Who can use it

SwiftCart supports shopper, merchant, and owner accounts. The same login system supports password sign-in, OTP-based account access, and recovery workflows when needed.

How we reduce confusion

SwiftCart uses its own branding, policy pages, and support information so visitors can tell they are on an official SwiftCart screen rather than a copied marketplace sign-in form.

What information SwiftCart uses

  • Account details such as name, email address, mobile number, and delivery address.
  • Authentication data such as password hashes, one-time passwords, and login metadata used to protect accounts.
  • Order, wishlist, and product interaction data needed to run the marketplace features.
